Hi, I'm Ben Hoover

I build efficient, interpretable, and robust AI from memory, energy, and dynamics

I'm a researcher at Prefrontal in London, and I earned my Machine Learning PhD at Georgia Tech with Polo Chau. My goal is to make energy-based AI practical at the scale of modern workloads. My research has been an oral at ICML (top 1%) and a spotlight at NeurIPS (top 3%), and CRAM, my latest model, learns to reason 1,000x faster than looped models.

1980s physics, trained to reason in milliseconds

Benjamin Hoover, Hugh Blayney, James C. R. Whittington

  • Viral on X (2.1k ❤)

ICML'25

Tutorial on Associative Memory

A modern tour of Associative Memory

Dmitry Krotov, Benjamin Hoover, Parikshit Ram, Bao Pham

Energy-based Associative Memory transformed the field of AI, but it is hardly understood. We present a birds eye view of Associative Memory, beginning with the invention of the Hopfield Network and concluding with modern, dense storage versions that have strong connections to Transformers and kernel methods.

ICML'25

ConceptAttention

Diffusion transformers secretly segment concepts

Alec Helbling, Tuna Han Salih Meral, Benjamin Hoover, Pinar Yanardag, Polo Chau

  • Oral (top 1%)
  • Best Paper, Workshop@CVPR'25

We discover how to extract highly salient features from the learned representations of Diffusion Transformers, using our technique to segment images by semantic concept. We name our technique ConceptAttention, which outperforms all prior methods on single- and multi-class classification.

NeurIPS'24

DenseAMs meet Random Features

Add memories without adding parameters

Benjamin Hoover, Polo Chau, Hendrik Strobelt, Parikshit Ram, Dmitry Krotov

DenseAMs can store an exponential number of memories, but each memory adds new parameters to the energy function. We propose a novel, "Distributed representation for DenseAMs" (DrDAM) that allows us to add new memories without increasing the total number of parameters.
